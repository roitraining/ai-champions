**Status values:**

- **Enforced**: a control holds the rule, and you can show the evidence (a settings file, a hook, a log).
- **Reviewed**: a person checks it (a pull request review, a checklist), but nothing blocks a mistake.
- **Undecided**: the team has not decided yet. Leave it that way until it is real.
